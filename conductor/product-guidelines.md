# FlashMath Product Guidelines

## Brand Voice & Tone
- **Encouraging & Energetic:** Celebrate personal growth, speed milestones, and accuracy streaks without being patronizing or overly juvenile.
- **Concise & Direct:** Keep instructions, labels, and feedback brief. In timed drills, every millisecond counts—avoid verbose dialogs or visual clutter.
- **Constructive in Failure:** When mistakes happen, display the correct product clearly as a rapid learning cue rather than a punitive buzzer.

## Visual Design & Aesthetics
- **Distraction-Free Minimalist Interface:** Clean layout prioritizing the flashcard and active problem. Remove extraneous navigation during active drills.
- **Large, High-Legibility Typography:** Use clear monospace or tabular-figure sans-serif fonts for arithmetic numbers to avoid layout jitter as numbers change.
- **High Contrast & Theme Support:** Crisp contrast ratios meeting WCAG AAA standards for reading ease, with seamless Light and Dark mode options.
- **Punchy, Micro-Feedback:** Subtle green and red pulse accents or micro-animations indicating instant correctness without disorienting the user.

## UX & Interaction Principles
- **Keyboard-First & Numpad Ergonomics:**
  - Auto-focus the input immediately upon round start.
  - Full support for both physical numpads, standard number rows, and touchscreen on-screen numeric keypads.
- **Frictionless Flow:**
  - Fast restart via hotkeys (e.g., Space or Enter to restart a sprint).
  - No modal interruptions during an active 3-minute challenge.
- **Accessibility & Tactile Feedback:**
  - Full keyboard navigability across all menus and setting selectors.
  - Semantic HTML structure and ARIA live regions for screen readers.
  - Optional subtle audio cues (toggleable) for correct answers, mistakes, and sprint countdown.
